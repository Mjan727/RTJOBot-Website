# RTJOBot Privacy Policy **Last updated: August 28, 2026** RTJOBot ("the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") is a Discord bot designed to provide moderation, Roblox verification, group/role management, and related functionality for Discord servers. This Privacy Policy explains what information RTJOBot may process and how that information is used. ## 1. Information We Process Depending on the features used by a Discord server, RTJOBot may process the following information: ### Discord Information * Discord User ID * Discord Server (Guild) ID * Discord role and permission information * Discord username and display information when required for bot functionality ### Roblox Information When you use RTJOBot's Roblox verification system, RTJOBot may receive information provided through Roblox OAuth, including: * Roblox User ID * Roblox username/display name * Roblox account information made available through the OAuth authorization * Roblox group membership and role information when required for verification ### Server Configuration For servers using RTJOBot, the Bot may store configuration information such as: * Configured verification settings * Roblox group IDs * Discord role IDs * Group-to-role bindings * Moderation configuration * Channel IDs used for logging or bot functionality ## 2. How We Use Information Information processed by RTJOBot is used to provide and maintain its functionality, including: * Verifying Roblox accounts with Discord accounts * Checking Roblox group membership and roles * Assigning or managing Discord roles based on Roblox groups * Providing moderation functionality * Recording moderation actions when logging features are enabled * Maintaining server-specific Bot configuration * Preventing abuse and misuse of the Bot * Troubleshooting and improving the Bot We do not use information collected through RTJOBot to sell or advertise to users. ## 3. Roblox OAuth RTJOBot may use Roblox's OAuth system to allow users to connect their Roblox account with their Discord account. When you authorize RTJOBot through Roblox OAuth, Roblox may provide information according to the permissions requested during authorization. You can revoke authorization through the appropriate Roblox account settings. RTJOBot only requests information necessary for the functionality being provided. ## 4. Data Sharing We do not sell personal information. Information processed by RTJOBot may be accessible to the operators of the Discord server where the Bot is installed when that information is necessary for the Bot's functionality. Information may also be processed by third-party services required to operate the Bot or its infrastructure. ## 5. Data Retention Information may be retained for as long as it is necessary to provide RTJOBot's functionality or maintain server configuration. Server administrators may remove RTJOBot from their server to stop the Bot from processing information for that server. If you would like to request deletion of information associated with RTJOBot, please contact us using the contact method provided below. ## 6. Data Security We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by RTJOBot. However, no online service can guarantee complete security. ## 7. Children's Privacy RTJOBot is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. ## 8. Third-Party Services RTJOBot interacts with services including Discord and Roblox. Their own privacy policies and terms may also apply when you use those services. RTJOBot is not affiliated with or endorsed by Roblox Corporation or Discord Inc. unless explicitly stated otherwise. ## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. ## 10. Contact If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or information processed by RTJOBot, please contact the RTJOBot development team through the official RTJOBot support/contact channel. **RTJOBot**
